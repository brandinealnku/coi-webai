webAI experience standard

Every experience has to earn the AI.

These are the product constraints carried forward from the UX, accessibility, trust, and human-AI research.

1 · Value before permission

Explain the outcome first. Ask for camera, microphone, or other sensors only at the moment they become useful.

2 · System state stays visible

Waiting, loading, listening, looking, processing, result, and error states are explicit—not implied.

3 · Every AI path has a human path

Tap, keyboard, and manual alternatives preserve task completion when sensing fails or users decline it.

4 · Limits are part of the interface

Outputs are framed as detections or inferences, not human-like understanding. Uncertainty is shown where useful.

5 · One meaningful outcome

Each experience has a clear human takeaway or action instead of stacking unrelated AI tricks.

6 · The buyer can prove value

Activation, completion, recovery, and outcome metrics are defined before additional platform complexity is built.

How this prototype works

Camera: access is requested only after you choose the camera path. The camera feed stays in the page and is analyzed by a browser-loaded object-detection model. This prototype does not intentionally record or upload camera frames.

Voice: access begins only after you choose the voice path. Browser speech-recognition implementations may use platform or cloud speech services. If you prefer not to use that, choose the tap path.

AI model: the camera path loads TensorFlow.js and COCO-SSD from a public CDN. The model is useful for demonstrating object recognition, not for identifying people or understanding personal meaning.

Accessibility + recovery:

  • Keyboard-accessible controls and visible focus states.
  • Tap/manual path preserves the core experience without camera or microphone.
  • Reduced-motion preferences are honored.
  • Status changes use text, not color alone.

This is a prototype experience, not a production privacy policy. A client deployment should document its own data flow, hosting, analytics, retention, and accessibility testing.
