Google Sheets is the shared research dashboard for visitor and observer event data.

Open the research workbook

Visitor and observer interactions are submitted as event rows through Google Apps Script. Use the Sheet for filtering, summaries, charts, and post-event analysis.

Recommended analysis

Review unique visitor sessions, completion, scan success, recognized object, scan time, total experience time, noticed-something-new responses, observation choice, follow-connection intent, observer help, visible attention to the physical object, attention, companion talk, and abandonment.

Observer records automatically pair to the visitor session by recognized object and active-session timing. The Study Code remains only as a fallback.
