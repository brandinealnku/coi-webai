Open the research workbook

Visitor and observer interactions are submitted as event rows through Google Apps Script. Use the Sheet for filtering, summaries, charts, and post-event analysis.

Recommended analysis

Review unique visitor sessions, completion, scan success, recognized object, scan time, total experience time, look-closer responses, observation choice, desired next action, observer help, visible look-back behavior, attention, companion talk, and abandonment.

Important: Observer records are standalone timestamped observations and are not automatically linked one-to-one with visitor sessions.
