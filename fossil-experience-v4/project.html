ABOUT THE PROJECT

What are we testing?

Expand Your World is an NKU AI Native Studio prototype created with Cincinnati Museum Center to explore whether a small digital interaction can help visitors notice more in the physical exhibit—and then motivate them to keep exploring.

  • Scan: identify a featured object or proxy.
  • Notice: look for one specific clue.
  • Discover: uncover one short interpretation.
  • Continue: follow the story to the next stop.

Why AI Day?

This AI Day version is a rehearsal. The team is testing the visitor flow, interface, physical wayfinding, and object-recognition approach before the final museum experience is complete.

Who built it?

Students in Northern Kentucky University’s AI Native Studio, working with faculty and Cincinnati Museum Center partners.
